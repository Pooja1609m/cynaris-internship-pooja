# Retail Sales KPI Dashboard – Performance Narrative

## 1. Introduction

This report explains the key business insights obtained from the Retail Sales KPI Dashboard created using Power BI. The dashboard focuses on revenue, profit, orders, quantity sold, and sales trends.

## 2. Overall Sales Performance

Total Revenue and Total Orders help evaluate the overall sales performance of the business. These KPIs provide an overview of the revenue generated and the number of sales transactions completed.

## 3. Profitability Analysis

Total Profit and Profit Margin help measure the profitability of the business. Monitoring these indicators helps management understand whether sales are generating sufficient profit.

## 4. Monthly Sales Trends

The Monthly Sales Trend chart helps identify changes in revenue over time. Monthly increases may indicate improving sales performance, while decreases may require further investigation.

## 5. Category-wise Performance

The Category-wise Revenue chart compares the revenue generated by different product categories. It helps identify high-performing categories and areas that may need improvement.

## 6. Region-wise Performance

The Region-wise Profit chart compares profit across different regions. This helps management identify strong-performing regions and investigate regions with lower profitability.

## 7. Recommendations

- Monitor revenue and profit regularly.
- Investigate months with declining sales.
- Focus on improving underperforming product categories.
- Review regional performance to identify improvement opportunities.
- Use the date slicer to compare performance across different periods.

## 8. Conclusion

The Power BI KPI dashboard provides a simple and interactive way to monitor retail business performance. It helps managers understand sales trends, profitability and regional performance, supporting better data-driven decisions.
