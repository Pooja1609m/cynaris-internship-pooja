# Retail Business KPI Framework

## 1. Business Objective
To monitor retail business performance by measuring sales, orders, quantity sold, regional performance and category performance.

## 2. KPI Definitions

| No. | KPI | Formula | Owner | Frequency | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total Sales Revenue | SUM(Sales) | Sales Manager | Monthly | ₹5,00,000 |
| 2 | Total Orders | COUNT DISTINCT(Order ID) | Operations Manager | Monthly | 500 |
| 3 | Units Sold | SUM(Quantity) | Inventory Manager | Monthly | 1,000 |
| 4 | Average Order Value | Total Sales / Total Orders | Finance Manager | Monthly | ₹1,000 |
| 5 | Monthly Sales Growth | ((Current Sales - Previous Sales) / Previous Sales) × 100 | Sales Manager | Monthly | 10% |
| 6 | Regional Sales | SUM(Sales) by Region | Regional Manager | Weekly | Meet regional target |
| 7 | Category Sales | SUM(Sales) by Category | Category Manager | Weekly | Meet category target |
| 8 | Average Units per Order | Total Quantity / Total Orders | Operations Manager | Monthly | 2 units |
| 9 | Daily Sales Run Rate | Month-to-date Sales / Elapsed Days | Sales Manager | Daily | ₹16,667 |
| 10 | Daily Order Run Rate | Month-to-date Orders / Elapsed Days | Operations Manager | Daily | 17 orders |

*Targets are illustrative and should be adjusted using actual historical business data.*

## 3. Leading vs Lagging KPIs

### Leading Indicators
1. Daily Sales Run Rate
2. Daily Order Run Rate
3. Current-period Units Sold

### Lagging Indicators
1. Total Sales Revenue
2. Total Orders
3. Average Order Value
4. Monthly Sales Growth
5. Regional Sales
6. Category Sales
7. Average Units per Order

## 4. SMART KPI Example

Increase monthly sales revenue by 10% within three months.

- Specific: Increase sales revenue.
- Measurable: 10% growth.
- Achievable: Based on historical performance.
- Relevant: Supports business growth.
- Time-bound: Within three months.

## 5. Business Benefits

- Monitors business performance.
- Identifies high-performing regions.
- Tracks popular product categories.
- Supports better sales planning.
- Helps managers make data-driven decisions.

## 6. Conclusion

A KPI framework helps retail companies measure progress towards business goals. Tracking sales, orders, quantity and regional performance helps managers identify problems and improve business decisions.
